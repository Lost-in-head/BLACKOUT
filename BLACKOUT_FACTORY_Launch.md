## IDEA-TO-MARKET WORKFLOW

### TOTAL TIME: 30 DAYS (4-5 weeks)

```
Week 1 (Days 1-7): DISCOVERY & VALIDATION
├── Day 1-2: RESEARCH validates market ($1M+ TAM)
├── Day 3-4: PRODUCT defines problem and users
├── Day 5-7: ENGINEERING designs architecture
└── APPROVAL: APEX gives go/no-go

Week 2 (Days 8-14): DESIGN & PLANNING
├── Day 8-9: DESIGNER creates wireframes
├── Day 10-11: DISCOVERY validates with users
├── Day 12-14: ENGINEERING finalizes architecture
└── APPROVAL: Design signed off

Week 3 (Days 15-21): DEVELOPMENT & TESTING
├── Day 15-19: ENGINEERING builds core features
├── Day 20-21: QUALITY runs full test suite
└── APPROVAL: Code ready for production

Week 4 (Days 22-28): LAUNCH PREPARATION
├── Day 22-24: MARKETING prepares launch
├── Day 25: DEPLOY prepares infrastructure
├── Day 26-27: SALES pre-sells to early customers
├── Day 28: LAUNCH to production
└── APPROVAL: Product live

Week 5+ (Days 29+): SCALE & OPTIMIZE
├── Day 29-30: ANALYST tracks metrics
├── Day 31+: FARMER expands accounts, OPTIMIZE improves performance
└── CONTINUOUS: SUPPORT manages customer success
```
