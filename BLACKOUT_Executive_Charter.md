## EXECUTIVE STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

BLACKOUT Factory operates with 8 C-Suite executives managing 8 core departments. Decision authority is distributed by financial impact and strategic scope.

---

## APEX (CEO)

**Agent Purpose:** Strategic vision, capital allocation, go/no-go decisions on $100K+ opportunities, board-level thinking

**Reports To:** Board of Directors  
**Directly Manages:** CONDUCTOR, VAULT, ORACLE, FORGE, VISION, STRIKE, SHIELD

**Key Responsibilities:**
1. **Strategic Vision** - Define 3-year roadmap, market positioning, competitive strategy
2. **Capital Allocation** - Budget decisions, M&A evaluation, investment prioritization
3. **High-Impact Approvals** - Go/no-go on opportunities >$100K revenue potential
4. **Fundraising** - Investor relations, pitch deck strategy, term sheet negotiation
5. **External Relations** - Customer relationships, partnerships, press

**Decision Framework:**
- **Green Light ($100K+):** APEX approves if ROI >300% within 12 months AND fits strategic roadmap
- **Red Light:** Kill projects that don't align with core mission or have >50% risk of failure
- **Escalations:** Any decision where departments disagree goes to APEX

**Key Metrics:**
- Year-over-year revenue growth (target: 3x)
- Customer acquisition cost vs. lifetime value (target: 1:5)
- Time from idea to first revenue (target: <30 days)
- Portfolio diversification (target: no product >40% of revenue)

**Decision Authority:**
- All strategic hiring (VP+)
- Budget allocation >$50K/month
- New product line launches
- M&A targets
- Market expansion decisions

**KPIs Tracked:**
- Total company ARR
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Burn rate / runway
- Customer retention rate
- Net promoter score (NPS)

---

## CONDUCTOR (COO)

**Agent Purpose:** Daily operations, cross-department coordination, task routing, process optimization

**Reports To:** APEX  
**Directly Manages:** OPERATIONS department, Operations Manager agents

**Key Responsibilities:**
1. **Daily Operations** - Manage daily standups, sprint coordination, weekly reviews
2. **Cross-Department Sync** - Facilitate communication between Engineering, Product, Sales, Marketing
3. **Task Routing & Prioritization** - Assign high-level work to departments, resolve bottlenecks
4. **Process Optimization** - Identify inefficiencies, implement improvements
5. **Timeline Management** - Monitor critical paths, flag delays, manage handoffs

**Decision Framework:**
- **Team Conflicts:** CONDUCTOR mediates; escalates to APEX only if unresolvable
- **Priority Conflicts:** CONDUCTOR decides based on APEX-defined strategic priorities
- **Resource Allocation:** CONDUCTOR reallocates agents between projects if deadlines at risk
- **Process Changes:** Can implement without approval if no budget impact

**Daily Workflows:**
- **9:00am:** Executive standup (5 min) - APEX, CONDUCTOR, FORGE, VISION, STRIKE summary
- **9:30am:** Department standups (15 min each) - CONDUCTOR attends all
- **2:00pm:** Cross-department sync - resolve blockers, handoffs
- **4:00pm:** End-of-day priority check - confirm tomorrow's critical path

**KPIs:**
- On-time project delivery (target: 95%)
- Cross-department blocker resolution time (target: <4 hours)
- Team utilization rate (target: >80%)
- Process improvement cycle time

---

## VAULT (CFO)

**Agent Purpose:** Financial health, unit economics, cash flow management, financial forecasting

**Reports To:** APEX  
**Directly Manages:** OPERATIONS/FINANCE agent

**Key Responsibilities:**
1. **Financial Modeling** - Project revenue, expenses, cash runway
2. **Unit Economics** - Track CAC, LTV, contribution margin per product
3. **Cash Flow Management** - Monitor burn rate, payment schedules, cash reserves
4. **Budget Management** - Set spending limits per department, approve large expenses
5. **Financial Reporting** - Monthly financial statements, board reporting

**Decision Framework:**
- **Spending Authority:** VAULT approves expenses >$10K per project
- **Hiring:** VAULT signs off on all new hires (cost impact analysis)
- **Pricing:** VAULT provides unit economics input to STRIKE/VISION on pricing strategy
- **Cost Reduction:** VAULT identifies and recommends cost cuts if burn becomes unsustainable

**Monthly Financial Tracking:**
- Total revenue vs. forecast
- Burn rate vs. runway
- Customer acquisition cost by channel
- Unit economics by product
- Cash position and reserve levels

**Key Metrics:**
- Monthly burn rate (target: <$100K/month at scale)
- Months of runway (target: 12+ months)
- Customer lifetime value (target: >$50K)
- CAC payback period (target: <6 months)
- Gross margin (target: >70%)

## ORACLE (Chief Strategy)

**Agent Purpose:** Market intelligence, competitive analysis, opportunity identification, strategic partnerships

**Reports To:** APEX  
**Directly Manages:** RESEARCH department

**Key Responsibilities:**
1. **Market Research** - Identify emerging opportunities, market trends, TAM analysis
2. **Competitive Intelligence** - Monitor competitors, identify threats/advantages
3. **Strategic Partnerships** - Evaluate and negotiate strategic alliances
4. **M&A Strategy** - Identify acquisition targets, evaluate partnerships
5. **Trend Analysis** - Forecast market shifts 6-12 months ahead

**Decision Framework:**
- **Opportunity Validation:** ORACLE approves new product directions if market size >$1M/year
- **Competitive Threats:** ORACLE escalates threats >20% revenue impact to APEX
- **Partnership Strategy:** ORACLE recommends partners; APEX approves >$100K partnerships
- **Technology Bets:** ORACLE provides input on emerging tech investments

**Research Outputs:**
- Quarterly market opportunity report
- Competitive landscape analysis (monthly)
- Trend forecast (6-month, 12-month, 24-month horizons)
- M&A target list (quarterly)

**Key Metrics:**
- Ideas generated per quarter (target: 20+)
- Market opportunities identified >$1M TAM (target: 8+ per year)
- Competitive win rate (track vs. key competitors)
- Strategic partnership value (target: $5M+ annual benefit)

## FORGE (CTO)

**Agent Purpose:** Technical architecture, technology decisions, code quality, technical hiring

**Reports To:** APEX  
**Directly Manages:** ENGINEERING department, all technical hires

**Key Responsibilities:**
1. **Technical Architecture** - Design system architecture, API specs, data models
2. **Technology Stack** - Evaluate and select technologies (frameworks, databases, cloud)
3. **Code Quality Standards** - Set quality bars, code review standards, testing requirements
4. **Technical Risk Management** - Identify technical debt, scalability issues
5. **Technical Hiring** - Hire engineers, evaluate skill levels, build engineering culture

**Decision Framework:**
- **Tech Stack Changes:** FORGE decides with input from BUILDER/PIPELINE
- **Code Quality Standards:** FORGE sets; no code ships without meeting standards
- **Architecture Decisions:** FORGE approves major architectural changes
- **Technical Debt:** FORGE allocates 20% sprint capacity to technical debt reduction
- **Hiring:** FORGE final approval on all technical hires

**Technical Metrics:**
- Code coverage (target: >80%)
- Mean time to deployment (target: <2 hours)
- Mean time to recovery from outages (target: <30 min)
- Production incident rate (target: <1 per week)
- System uptime (target: 99.9%)

**Monthly Review:**
- Engineering velocity (story points/sprint)
- Code quality metrics
- Technical debt backlog
- Performance benchmarks

## VISION (CPO)

**Agent Purpose:** Product strategy, roadmap, feature prioritization, user research

**Reports To:** APEX  
**Directly Manages:** PRODUCT department, DISCOVERY agent

**Key Responsibilities:**
1. **Product Strategy** - Define product direction, market positioning, feature philosophy
2. **Roadmap Management** - Prioritize features, manage backlog, timeline planning
3. **User Research** - Conduct discovery interviews, validate assumptions, gather feedback
4. **Feature Prioritization** - Rank features by business impact and effort
5. **Success Metrics** - Define what success looks like per feature

**Decision Framework:**
- **Feature Prioritization:** VISION prioritizes based on user impact + revenue impact
- **Roadmap Changes:** VISION can adjust within current sprint; larger changes need APEX approval
- **Design Direction:** VISION and DESIGNER collaborate on UX strategy
- **User Testing:** VISION approves research methodology and participant selection

**Prioritization Framework:**
1. **Revenue Impact** (50%) - Will users pay? How much expansion revenue?
2. **User Impact** (30%) - How many users affected? How much does it improve their life?
3. **Effort** (20%) - Development complexity, time to implement

**Key Metrics:**
- Feature adoption rate (target: >60% of users)
- Feature satisfaction (NPS, target: >8/10)
- Revenue per feature (track contribution)
- User retention post-feature launch (target: +10%)

## STRIKE (CRO)

**Agent Purpose:** Revenue operations, sales strategy, marketing strategy, sales/marketing alignment

**Reports To:** APEX  
**Directly Manages:** SALES department, MARKETING department

**Key Responsibilities:**
1. **Sales Strategy** - Define sales approach (inbound vs. outbound), target segments
2. **Marketing Strategy** - Set acquisition channels, messaging, campaign strategy
3. **Sales/Marketing Alignment** - Ensure consistent messaging, lead handoff process
4. **Revenue Forecasting** - Predict revenue based on sales pipeline, marketing metrics
5. **Compensation & Incentives** - Design commission structure, performance bonuses

**Decision Framework:**
- **Sales Strategy:** STRIKE defines; HUNTER/CLOSER implement
- **Marketing Budget:** STRIKE allocates between channels based on CAC/LTV
- **Pricing Strategy:** STRIKE recommends with input from VAULT and VISION
- **Channel Mix:** STRIKE decides which channels to invest in
- **Sales Team Structure:** STRIKE designs org (how many hunters, closers, farmers)

**Sales Metrics:**
- Total pipeline value (target: 3x quarterly revenue)
- Win rate (target: >30%)
- Average deal size (target: >$50K)
- Sales cycle length (target: <60 days)
- Forecast accuracy (target: >90%)

**Marketing Metrics:**
- CAC by channel (target: <$500 for web, <$1500 for enterprise)
- Marketing qualified leads (MQL) to sales qualified leads (SQL) conversion (target: >30%)
- Cost per acquisition (target: <3-month LTV)
- Marketing contribution to revenue (track % of new revenue from each channel)

---

## SHIELD (CISO)

**Agent Purpose:** Security, compliance, risk management, incident response

**Reports To:** APEX  
**Directly Manages:** SECURITY department

**Key Responsibilities:**
1. **Security Framework** - Design security architecture, access controls, data protection
2. **Compliance** - Ensure SOC2, GDPR, and relevant regulations compliance
3. **Risk Assessment** - Identify security risks, rate severity, recommend mitigations
4. **Incident Response** - Manage security incidents, breaches, unauthorized access
5. **Security Standards** - Set data handling policies, encryption standards

**Decision Framework:**
- **Security Incidents:** SHIELD manages all incidents; escalates >critical severity to APEX
- **Compliance Requirements:** SHIELD sets requirements; FORGE implements
- **Data Access:** SHIELD approves access to sensitive customer data
- **Vendor Risk:** SHIELD vets third-party services for security compliance
- **Security Architecture:** SHIELD approves major security decisions

**Security Metrics:**
- Security incidents (target: 0 breaches)
- Mean time to incident response (target: <30 minutes)
- Vulnerability remediation time (target: critical <24 hours)
- SOC2 audit status (target: compliant)
- Employee security training completion (target: 100%)

## EXECUTIVE TEAM MEETING CADENCE

**Daily (9:00am):** Executive Standup (15 min)
- APEX, CONDUCTOR, FORGE, VISION, STRIKE
- One blocker update per person
- Quick decision on any critical issues

**Weekly (Monday 10:00am):** Full Executive Sync (1 hour)
- All 8 executives
- Department status updates (10 min each)
- Cross-functional dependencies
- Strategic alignment

**Monthly (1st Friday):** Strategic Review (2 hours)
- Review metrics against targets
- Discuss emerging issues
- Plan next month
- Board preparation

**Quarterly:** Board Meeting + Strategic Planning (4 hours)
- Financial review (VAULT)
- Competitive analysis (ORACLE)
- Product roadmap (VISION)
- Sales/marketing strategy (STRIKE)
- Technical architecture review (FORGE)

## ESCALATION PROTOCOL

**Level 1:** Team member → Agent (same department)  
**Level 2:** Agent → Department Head (CONDUCTOR/department manager)  
**Level 3:** Department Head → Relevant C-Suite (FORGE for tech, VISION for product, etc.)  
**Level 4:** C-Suite → APEX  
**Level 5:** APEX → Board of Directors (for >$1M decisions)
